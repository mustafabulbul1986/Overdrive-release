Follow my phone over USB
Reads a small BLE scanner plugged into the car's USB port and publishes a "Phone Proximity" signal your automations can use. It does not lock or unlock anything by itself.

When is the phone near, and when is it away
Stand where you want the car to react and watch the Live tab, then set the thresholds around what you see. Leave a gap of at least 10 dBm between them, or the signal will flip back and forth on the spot. Readings that are too few, in between, or missing never change the answer — the phone being out of range is not the same as being away.
Which beacon to follow
These come from whatever app broadcasts the beacon on your phone. The scanner only reports this one, so nothing else nearby is listened to.
